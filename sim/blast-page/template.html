What to trust

  • Volume in the break zone: worked out from the charges and a published rock-breaking limit, with no fitting. The same method read 23% high on SWELLFACTOR, so 21,900 m³ is more likely high than low.
  • Pile and spread: the figures assume the central break zone, and they also depend strongly on friction, which is a model setting here and hasn't been measured. Piles also stand steeper than real muck.
  • Fragment sizes: an uncalibrated estimate. Median size runs from 11 to 50 cm depending on the rock factor, this powder factor is above the cases the method was checked against, and boulders are placed at random because no source gave a location rule.
  • Far-flying blocks: don't read these as flyrock distances. The model isn't built or checked for them, and the paper launch sends some implausibly far.
  • Not measured at Carraizo: rock strength, density, joints, hole pattern, charge, and timing.
